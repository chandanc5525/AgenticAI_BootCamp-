## Suggested capstone: Agentic Business Intelligence Platform

A user asks a business question. A supervisor agent coordinates:
- Research agent: gathers approved external/internal context.
- Data analyst agent: analyses supplied structured data with controlled tools.
- Knowledge agent: retrieves internal documents using RAG.
- Reporting agent: produces an evidence-backed report.

Include source attribution, human approval for consequential actions, evaluation, logging, authentication, and deployment.
